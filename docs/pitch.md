# The Freundebuch Pitch

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 05.06.2026
**Status:** Active

How to explain Freundebuch to friends, family, and anyone who asks "so what are you building?" - in one line, thirty seconds, or a proper conversation.

A German version lives at [pitch.de.md](./pitch.de.md). It's an idiomatic translation, not a literal one - use whichever fits the conversation.

---

## The One-Liner

> "It's a friendship book for grown-ups - remember everything about the people you care about, and never lose touch."

---

## The Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

> "Remember the Freundebuch from kindergarten? Everyone filled out a page - favorite color, dream job, hobbies. As adults we meet way more people, but we stopped writing things down. Freundebuch brings that back: every person in your life gets a page. Their kids' names, how you met, what you talked about last time, their birthday. It syncs with the contacts on your phone, so it's not *another* address book - it's your address book, with a memory. And it runs on my own server, so nobody's mining your friendships for ads."

---

## The Story Pitch (for a real conversation)

**Hook:**

> "When did you last forget a birthday? Or blank on someone's partner's name mid-conversation?"

**Problem:**

> "Your phone's address book stores phone numbers. That's it. Everything that actually matters about a person - how you met, what their kids are called, that they switched jobs, that you owe them a coffee - lives in your head. And heads leak."

**Solution:**

> "Freundebuch gives every person a page, like the kindergarten books. After you catch up with someone, you jot down two lines. Next time you meet, you glance at their page and you're instantly the person who *remembers*. People notice that. It feels like care, because it is care - just with backup."

**Differentiator:**

> "And it understands groups. Add a family - say who's the parent, who's the kid - and it figures out all the relationships between them automatically. Add a colleague to a company, and everyone there becomes a colleague. No other app does that."

**Privacy close:**

> "It's not a startup that'll sell your data or shut down. Open source, runs on hardware you control, and your contacts sync via the same open standard your phone already speaks."

## Audience-Specific Hooks

Different people care about different things. Lead with what lands.

| Audience | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Non-technical friend | The birthday/memory pain. "Be the person who remembers." Then show a friend's page on your phone |
| Privacy-conscious | "Google knows your contacts. This keeps them on a server we control" |
| Tech person | Self-hosted, AGPL, SvelteKit/Hono/PostgreSQL + PostGIS, CardDAV with vCard 4.0, MCP server for AI assistants, weekly releases |
| Power user | "On the desktop you barely touch the mouse - search, open, and edit everything with keyboard shortcuts." Then demo it: a few keystrokes from search to a friend's page |
| Monica refugee | "Monica v4 is frozen, v5 is a multi-year beta with no migration path. Freundebuch is actively developed" - see [comparison-monica.md](./comparison-monica.md) |
| Family / partner | Shared knowledge: "we both remember which cousin has the nut allergy" |

## Objection Handling

**"Isn't tracking your friends a bit creepy?"**

> "Is a paper address book creepy? Writing down a birthday isn't surveillance - it's the opposite of treating people as disposable. Creepy is when a corporation does it *to* you. This is you, privately, caring on purpose."

**"I just use my phone contacts."**

> "You keep them - Freundebuch syncs with them. It just adds the layer your phone can't: the human stuff."

**"Another app I'll download and abandon?"**

> "It works through the contacts app you already use. The web app is for when you want the richer view."

**"Sounds like a sales tool."**

> "It's deliberately not. No pipelines, no leads. It's modeled on a children's friendship book, and it shows."

---

## Honest Caveats

Say these unprompted - honesty builds more trust than polish:

- It's a young project under active development. Reminders ("you haven't talked to Anna in three months") are on the roadmap but not built yet.
- It's self-hosted only right now - someone needs to run a server for you. (That someone is probably the person pitching.)
